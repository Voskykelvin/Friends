What We'll Be Doing

A packed afternoon of adventure, mud and good vibes. Start stretching now.

Mud Fun

The signature Njine Kabia experience. Slip, slide and get absolutely filthy. Extra clothes are not optional — this is a warning.

Zip Lining

Fly over the falls and forest canopy on the zip line. Heart-pumping and unforgettable — scream as loud as you want.

Bootcamp Activities

Physical challenges and obstacle courses. This is exactly why the organiser said start working out mapema mapema.

Team Building

Group challenges designed to bond the squad. You'll leave closer than you arrived. Promise.

Goat Feast & Picnic

A full goat eating session + brunch picnic by the falls. Come hungry. Leave full, muddy and grateful.

About Njine Kabia Falls

A stunning hidden gem in Kiabarikiri village, just outside Kerugoya town. Three cascading waterfalls, jungle trails and group activity grounds. About a 2-hour drive from Nairobi.
